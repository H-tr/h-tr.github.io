# Terms

Each survey has one term list. The list lives in the working notes of the survey
([workflow](workflow.md#working-notes)), not in the harness.

## Rules

- Use one term for one thing, with one meaning, in the survey, in the notes and in replies.
- Add a term to the list before the first use, with the meaning and the source, and tell the
  user in the same reply.
- Keep a "Not" column for words the user rejected and for words with a second meaning in the
  field.
- Define each term of the survey at the first use in the text. Assume only terms known to the
  whole field.
- When the literature uses one word for two opposite things, say so in the text and pick plain
  words for the survey.
- Names of systems, symbols and identifiers stay unchanged.
- Mark a term coined by the survey as coined. Do not attribute a coined term to a source.

## Format

| Term | Meaning | Not | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The term | One sentence | Rejected or confusing alternatives | A source, the formulation, or the user's rule with a date |
